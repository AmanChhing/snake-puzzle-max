Delete your data

Snake Puzzle Max · by NutanTech Labs · Last updated 27 November 2026

Snake Puzzle Max has no user accounts, so there is no account to delete. Here is each kind of data the app holds or sends, and how to remove it.

1. Data on your phone

What it is: your level progress, best times and stars, daily and weekly puzzle results, your streak, the game's own record of how you are playing (which it uses to make the next level fairer for you and to time any reminder you asked for), your settings, your reminders choice, your leaderboard nickname and the anonymous leaderboard ID. This record stays on your phone and is not sent to us or anyone else.

How to delete it: uninstall the app, or open your phone's Settings → Apps → Snake Puzzle Max → Storage → Clear storage. It is removed immediately and completely. We never hold a copy.

2. Leaderboard data

What it is: your nickname, your best time on each level you cleared, your number of levels cleared and total time, stored on Google Firebase under an anonymous random ID.

How to delete it:

  1. Open the app, tap Me, and note the name at the top (for example "Snake 4821").
  2. Email info@nutantechlabs.com with the subject "Snake Puzzle Max – delete my leaderboard data" and that nickname.
  3. We delete every leaderboard row for that nickname within 30 days and reply to confirm. Nothing is kept afterwards.

If you want to keep playing with a clean slate instead, you can simply change your nickname in the app (Me, then the pencil by your name).

3. Advertising data

What it is: your advertising identifier and ad interaction events, collected by Google AdMob to show ads. NutanTech Labs does not receive or store this data; Google does.

How to delete or limit it: reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings (Android: Settings → Google → Ads → Delete advertising ID). In the EU/UK you can also withdraw consent in the app's consent form. For data Google already holds, use myadcenter.google.com.

4. App analytics and crash reports

What it is: on Android, from version 1.0.6, Google Analytics for Firebase records how the app is used: sessions, screens viewed, ads shown and what they earned, and from version 1.0.7 gameplay events such as levels started and finished, hints used and daily puzzles played. From version 1.0.7, if the app crashes, Firebase Crashlytics sends a crash report to Google. Both are tied only to identifiers created for your installation, not to your name, email or any account. The privacy policy lists everything that is sent.

How to delete or limit it: because this data is not linked to you, we cannot pick out yours to delete it. Reinstalling the app gives it new identifiers, so nothing new is added to the old ones, and Google deletes the old data automatically: analytics after 14 months at most, crash reports after 90 days. In the EU/UK, the choice you make in the app's consent form also applies to analytics, and you can change it at any time. You can also reset your advertising ID (see section 3).

5. Reminders

What it is: if you turned reminders on, they are scheduled on your phone as local notifications. No server sends them and nothing about them leaves your phone, apart from a note in analytics (section 4) when you open the app from one.

How to stop them: in the app, tap Me and switch Reminders off, or turn off the app's notifications in your phone's settings. Uninstalling the app removes them too.

What is kept

Nothing, once the steps above are done, apart from analytics and crash data, which Google deletes on its own after the periods in section 4. We keep no backups of leaderboard rows after deletion and no logs that identify you. Anonymous, aggregated counts (such as "how many players cleared level 10") contain no personal data and may remain.
