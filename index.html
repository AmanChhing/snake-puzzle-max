Simple rule, deep puzzle

How to play

Thirty seconds to learn.

  1. Look at the heads. Every snake points somewhere. A snake can leave only if the straight line in front of its head is clear all the way off the board.
  2. Tap a snake that can go. It slithers away along its own body. The space it leaves may free the next one.
  3. Mind your lives. Tap a blocked snake and it bumps into whatever is in front of it — that costs one of three lives.
  4. Clear the board. When the last snake is gone, the level is done and your time is recorded. On to the next.

Questions

Short answers. More on the support page.

Is it free?

Yes. The game is free and supported by ads. Ads appear between some levels, and you can choose to watch one to get a life back, a hint, or to skip to a level you have not reached.

Do I need an account?

No. Your progress is saved on your phone. The leaderboard uses a nickname you pick and an anonymous ID — no email, no password.

I ran out of lives. Did I lose my progress?

No. You can watch an ad for one more life and continue the same board, or restart the level. Levels you have cleared stay cleared.

How do I zoom?

Pinch with two fingers, or tap the zoom button in the corner of the board. When zoomed in, a small map appears at the top right — tap it to jump to any part of the board.

Can I replay a level?

Yes. Tap the level number at the top to open the level map. Cleared levels show your best time; tap one to play it again.
