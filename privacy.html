Snake Puzzle Max – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2 October 2026

Snake Puzzle Max ("the app") is a puzzle game made by Aman Kumar. This page explains what information the app uses and why.

What the app stores on your device

Your progress (levels cleared, best times, the level you are on), your settings (sound, theme), and the name you choose for the leaderboard. This stays on your phone. Deleting the app deletes it.

Advertising

The app shows ads through Google AdMob. Ads pay for the game. The AdMob SDK may collect your device's advertising ID, IP address, device type and app usage events to show and measure ads. Where the law requires it (for example in the EU and UK), the app asks for your consent before personalised ads are shown, and you can change your choice in the app's consent form at any time.

How Google uses this data: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites. You can reset or limit your advertising ID in your phone's settings.

Leaderboard (optional)

If the leaderboard is switched on in your version of the app, your chosen nickname, your level times and your level count are sent to Google Firebase so they can be ranked against other players. You are identified only by an anonymous random ID; no account, email or name is required. Your nickname and times are visible to other players on the leaderboard.

What the app does not do

  • No account or sign-in is required.
  • No contacts, photos, location or microphone access.
  • No selling of personal data.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from them.
