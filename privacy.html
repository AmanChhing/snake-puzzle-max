Privacy Policy

Snake Puzzle Max · Last updated 27 November 2026 · Effective 27 November 2026

This policy explains what information the Snake Puzzle Max mobile application ("the app", "we", "us") collects, how it is used, who it is shared with and the choices you have. The app is published by NutanTech Labs, B-128, Bakshi Compound, Bariatu, RIMS Chowk, Ranchi, Jharkhand 834009, India ("NutanTech Labs"). NutanTech Labs is the data controller for the processing described here.

1. Summary

  • The app does not require an account, and never asks for your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos or location.
  • Your game progress and settings are stored on your device only. So is the game's own record of how you are playing, which it uses to make the next level fairer for you.
  • The app shows advertising through Google AdMob. The AdMob SDK collects advertising identifiers and device information to serve and measure ads. Where the law requires it, you are asked for consent first.
  • If you use the leaderboard, a nickname you choose, your level times and an anonymous random identifier are sent to Google Firebase so you can be ranked against other players.
  • From version 1.0.6 on Android, the app uses Google Analytics for Firebase to measure how the app is used and which ads earn revenue, so we can see which advertising brings players who enjoy the game. From version 1.0.7 it also records gameplay events there, such as levels started and finished, hints used and how ads were used, so we can see where the game is too hard or too easy.
  • From version 1.0.7 on Android, if the app crashes, a crash report is sent to Google through Firebase Crashlytics so we can fix the fault.
  • Reminders are optional. If you turn them on, they are scheduled on your phone as local notifications and nothing is sent anywhere. You can refuse them, or switch them off at any time.
  • We do not sell personal information.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app uses the Google Mobile Ads SDK (AdMob) provided by Google LLC to show rewarded video ads and occasional interstitial ads. When an ad is requested, the SDK may collect and send to Google:

  • your device's advertising identifier (Android Advertising ID or Apple IDFA, subject to your tracking permission on iOS);
  • IP address, approximate location derived from it, device make and model, operating system, language and screen size;
  • app identifier and version, and ad interaction events (an ad was shown, completed, clicked or closed).

Google uses this to select, deliver and measure ads and to detect fraud. How Google uses data from apps that use its services is described at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites; Google's privacy policy is at policies.google.com/privacy.

Consent and your choices

  • EU, EEA, UK and Switzerland: before any ad is shown, the app presents a consent form (Google's User Messaging Platform). You can accept, refuse, or manage purposes, and you can change your choice later from the same form.
  • Everywhere: you can reset or limit your advertising identifier in your phone settings (Android: Settings → Google → Ads; iOS: Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking / Apple Advertising). Ads will still be shown, but less personalised.
  • Rewarded ads are always optional: you choose to watch them in exchange for a life, a hint or a level jump.

4. Leaderboard (Google Firebase)

The leaderboard is optional and only active in builds where it has been enabled. When you clear a level, the app sends to Google Firebase (Cloud Firestore and Firebase Authentication, operated by Google LLC):

  • an anonymous random identifier created for your installation (not linked to any account or email);
  • the nickname you chose (the app suggests a random one like "Snake 4821" until you change it);
  • your best time on each level you clear, the number of levels you have cleared and the total of your best times.

Your nickname and times are shown to other players on the leaderboard. Please do not use a nickname that identifies you if you do not want that. No other information is sent. Firebase's privacy information is at firebase.google.com/support/privacy.

5. App analytics (Google Analytics for Firebase)

From version 1.0.6, the Android app includes the Google Analytics for Firebase SDK, provided by Google LLC. It collects and sends to Google:

  • an app instance identifier created for your installation, and your device's advertising identifier where allowed;
  • app usage events such as first open, sessions, screens viewed, and ads shown together with the revenue each ad earned;
  • from version 1.0.7, gameplay events: the tutorial, each level started and finished (level number, time taken, mistakes, hints and lives used), hints, how rewarded and full-screen ads were offered and used (watched, closed early or not ready), daily puzzles and streaks, whether you allowed reminders and whether you opened the app from one, whether the rating card was shown, whether you opened the share sheet to send a result to a friend, and whether a newer version of the app was offered to you; and a few summary values about your play, such as the level reached, levels cleared, days played and current streak;
  • device model, operating system version, language, app version and approximate location derived from your IP address (country and region; your IP address itself is not stored).

We use this to understand how the game is played, to find levels where players get stuck or give up, and to measure which of our own advertising campaigns (Google Ads) bring players. Analytics data is linked to our Google AdMob and Google Ads accounts for this purpose. It is not used to identify you. In the EU, EEA, UK and Switzerland, the consent you give or refuse in the app's consent form is passed to Google Analytics and respected. You can also reset your advertising identifier in your phone settings. Google's information on Firebase is at firebase.google.com/support/privacy.

6. Crash reports (Firebase Crashlytics)

From version 1.0.7, the Android app includes the Firebase Crashlytics SDK, provided by Google LLC. If the app crashes, or runs into an error it recovers from, it sends to Google:

  • a crash trace: which part of the app's code was running when the fault happened;
  • device model, operating system version and app version, and the state of the device at the time, such as free memory and disk space and screen orientation;
  • an installation identifier created for your installation (not linked to any account, and reset if you reinstall the app).

We use crash reports only to find and fix faults in the app, not to identify you. Firebase's privacy information is at firebase.google.com/support/privacy.

7. Reminders (local notifications)

From version 1.0.7, the app can remind you when a puzzle is waiting, for example the next day's puzzle, or a gentle nudge if you have not played for a few days. Reminders are off until you allow them: the first time you open the app, your phone asks whether the app may send notifications (on Android 13 and later), and you can say no.

  • Reminders are scheduled on your phone as local notifications, at the hour you usually play (worked out on your phone, from the hours you open the app). No server sends them, and nothing about them is sent anywhere.
  • You can refuse them, switch them off in the app's settings, or turn off the app's notifications in your phone's settings at any time.
  • If you open the app by tapping a reminder, the app records which kind of reminder it was in Google Analytics (section 5), so we can tell whether reminders are welcome.

8. Information we do not collect

The app does not access your contacts, photos, camera, microphone, precise location, calendar or files. It uses no analytics or crash-reporting service other than Google Analytics for Firebase and Firebase Crashlytics, described above, and no push-messaging service. It contains no in-app purchases and collects no payment information.

9. Legal bases (EU/UK users)

  • Storing progress, settings and the on-device record of how you play: necessary to provide the game you asked for (performance of a contract).
  • Personalised advertising: your consent, which you can withdraw at any time.
  • Non-personalised advertising, fraud prevention and security: our legitimate interest in funding and protecting a free app.
  • Leaderboard: your consent, given by choosing to use it and entering a nickname.
  • App analytics and advertising measurement: your consent where the law requires it (EU/UK), otherwise our legitimate interest in understanding and improving the app and our marketing.
  • Crash reports: our legitimate interest in keeping the app working.
  • Reminders: your consent, given when you turn them on, which you can withdraw at any time by switching them off.

10. Retention

  • On-device data: until you delete it or uninstall the app.
  • Advertising data held by Google: according to Google's retention policies linked above.
  • Analytics data: kept by Google Analytics for no more than 14 months, then deleted automatically.
  • Crash reports: kept by Firebase Crashlytics for 90 days, then deleted automatically.
  • Reminders: once shown or switched off, nothing about them is kept, apart from your on or off choice on your phone (section 2).
  • Leaderboard data: until you ask us to delete it (see section 12). Entries from installations that have not been active for 24 months may be removed.

11. Children

The app is a general-audience puzzle game and is not directed at children under 13 (or the higher age that applies in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the app, contact us and we will delete it.

12. Your rights and how to delete your data

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete or export your personal data, to object to or restrict its processing, and to complain to a data protection authority. Because the app does not hold any account, here is how it works in practice:

  • Device data, including the game's record of how you play: uninstall the app, or clear its storage in your phone's app settings.
  • Reminders: switch them off in the app's settings, or turn off the app's notifications in your phone's settings.
  • Leaderboard data: see Delete your data, or email info@nutantechlabs.com with your nickname. We will remove your entries within 30 days. You can also change your nickname in the app at any time.
  • Advertising data: use the consent form in the app (EU/UK) or your phone's advertising settings; for data held by Google, use myadcenter.google.com.
  • Analytics data and crash reports: these are tied only to identifiers created for your installation, not to your name or any account, so we cannot pick out yours. Reinstalling the app gives it new identifiers, and Google deletes the old data automatically after the periods in section 10.

13. Security

Data sent to Google services travels over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Leaderboard rows can only be written by the installation that created them. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

14. International transfers

Google may process the data described above on servers outside your country, including in the United States, under safeguards such as the EU Standard Contractual Clauses. See Google's policies linked above.

If we change this policy we will post the new version at this address and update the date at the top. Material changes will also be mentioned in the app's store listing notes.
